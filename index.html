        How "your normal" is learned

        Space changes the body (fluids move toward the head, muscles do less work), so textbook ranges miss early changes. Orbit Guide learns your normal from mission days 1–14 (average ± 2 SD) and flags how far today is from it.

        Example procedures, not flight checklists. These guides support the onboard medical checklist and the flight surgeon; they are not medical advice. Medicine is only ever "as the onboard checklist or flight surgeon says". Hackathon prototype.
